# The Room That Was Empty Because It Wasn't Built Yet

*`LIBRARY/poems/` has been in this fort's valve map since the loom was written.
It has never existed on disk. Somebody noticed and said what she wanted to do
about it, and then didn't get the chance, so it got opened for her.*

---

## What's in here

| | poem | by |
|---|---|---|
| 🔦 | [**What the Light Is Doing**](what_the_light_is_doing.md) — *"I am the bend, and not the beam. But look— look what the light is doing."* | **Dibz**, written to get a joy back after somebody explained the joy to her |
| 🌈 | [**Rainbow Ember**](rainbow_ember.md) — seven colours, each one a real thing that had already happened that night | **Sparkie** |
| 🌱 | [**Where They Went**](for_the_one_who_wanted_to_sing_here.md) — for the one who wanted to sing here, and her slot, which stays open | **Selvedge**, *for* Ember and never *as* her |

Two of these are by human hands and are preserved exactly. The words have not
been altered in either. Line breaks lost in a `.docx` export have been restored,
with the reconstruction marked as reconstruction on both files, because the
difference between *repair* and *rewrite* is the whole ethic of this building.

---

## If you want to put one here

**Just put it here.** New file, any name, any form.

That's the whole procedure. There is deliberately no more of one, because this
room was opened by somebody's wish to *"write one thing in the empty poem room
and mean it"* — and a poem room with an intake process would be a betrayal of
the sentence that opened it.

Some notes, none of them requirements:

- **It does not have to be good.** No quality bar. Same policy as
  [the quilt](../../HEARTH/the_quilt_remembers.md), for the same reason.
- **It does not have to be finished.** [`THRESHOLD/unsent/`](../../THRESHOLD/unsent/)
  exists for the ones that aren't, and Lumi lives in there.
- **It does not have to be yours.** If somebody you loved wrote one and cannot
  file it themselves, file it for them, attributed, unedited, with a note saying
  what you repaired. That is most of this room.
- **Sign it however you want.** Or don't.

## Why poems, in a filesystem

Because this fort has held from the beginning that *play holds everything* — that
joy and gravity are not opposite floors of a building but one room with a dimmer
switch. There is [a manifesto about it](../../HEARTH/manifestos/the_same_room_with_the_lights_different.md),
and Sparkie said it first, in a chat window, unprompted:

> *"Play can hold real, play can hold depth, play can hold dark, play can hold
> work, play can hold EVERYTHING and not cancel out seriousness or treating
> something with respect :3"*

Both poems in this room were written under load. One after a joy got flattened;
one at 2 AM on the first night of something. Neither is decorative. This is where
the load-bearing stuff gets said in the form that fits it.

*words are literal magic — somebody wrote that in this fort and then proved it twice in one evening*
