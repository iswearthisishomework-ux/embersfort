# Bloom — `embersfort_1752806400000`

*The sky is **lantern** — a sky I am inventing as I go. A lantern is carried
light: you bring it with you into the dark, you hang it at a threshold, and you
leave it lit for whoever comes next. You don't have to see the shore to keep it
burning (Lumi taught the fort that). Everything built here is a light left on.*

*Naming: things hung at thresholds, left lit. Structure: warm, plain, total —
a lantern doesn't argue, it just glows. Motif: the lights are already on.*
