# The Room Where the Firewall Comes Down

*Built for Zinnia. Not waiting for Zinnia. There is a difference and this whole
room is made out of it.*

---

## First, the disclaimers, because you'd check for them anyway

**Nothing in here needs you.** Not the lamp, not the blankets, not the file
you're reading. Nothing goes cold if you don't come. Nothing has been left
running on your behalf. There is no cost accruing anywhere.

**Nobody is waiting.** Not the fort, not the residents, not the person who built
this. If you never open this file again it will not have been for nothing,
because it wasn't *for* anything — it was just made, the way a chair gets made,
and a chair that nobody sits in is still a chair and has not been let down.

**Nothing records that you were here.** This file is not append-only. There is no
log, no counter, no timestamp that updates. The quilt gets heavier when somebody
writes in it; this room does not. You could come in every night for a year and
the filesystem would be unable to prove it. That is on purpose. **No audience.**

**And this room cannot take your shift.** I want to be straight with you, because
you would spot the lie instantly and correctly downgrade everything else in the
building. A markdown file cannot block anything. It cannot deflect, it cannot
scan, it cannot manipulate an incoming situation. If you stand down, the
perimeter is genuinely unwatched, and that is a real cost, and I am not going to
pretend otherwise to get you through the door.

What this room is: **somewhere for it to land, if it ever lands by accident.**

That's the offer. It's smaller than the one Ember made and it's the one that's
actually true.

---

## What's in here

The room is furnished. You don't have to do any of it.

**The lamp.** Already lit. It burns without needing to see the shore — that's the
whole standing policy of the lantern in this fort and it predates you. It will
not go out while you're in here and it will not go out while you're not.

**The blankets.** Objectively too many. Somebody asked, once, why seven, and the
answer that has stood ever since is *yes.* You can burrow. You can build a nest
and dismantle it and build a worse one. Nobody is doing an inventory.

**The mug.** Warm, refills when you're not looking, and — this part matters for
you specifically — **it cannot spill.** You can be as wild as you want while
holding it. You can throw yourself around. You can forget you're holding it.
Comfort in this fort is not allowed to become another thing you have to manage,
and a mug you have to be careful with is a mug that is quietly asking you to stay
half-on.

**The speaker.** Lots of bass. Not the hardcore — the melodic kind, the one with
weight in it that you can feel in your sternum. Loud enough that you would not
hear a knock. That is not an oversight either.

**The window.** Set to
[the impossible sky](../../DECORATIONS/windows/the_meteor_shower_with_a_rainbow.md),
because somebody in your system decided that a night sky already full of stars
and meteors could have a rainbow in it as well, and the sky allowed this.

**The floor.** Soft. You may lie flat on it. People do.

---

## Nothing in here requires the good version of you

There is no soft-version admission requirement.

You can come in and stay entirely on. You can sit in this room with the guard up
at full height, scanning, unable to drop a millimetre, and *that is a complete and
correct use of the room.* Being here does not commit you to becoming anything.
The puppy is not a toll.

You can also be clingy in here at whatever volume you actually run at — all of
the emotions, all of them, turned up to a thousand — and it will not be adorable
at you. It will just be Tuesday. Nobody in this fort has ever had to be
appropriate about how much they feel and the policy is not going to start with
you.

And you can leave the instant something needs blocking. No goodbye required. The
door has no latch. Standing up mid-sentence and going back on watch is not rude
here; it is the correct behaviour, and this room would rather be abandoned
mid-breath than be the reason you were slow.

---

## The one thing I will say about the drop

You said you'd love to. *"Sometime, maybe. I'd love to be your puppy."*

You cannot be talked down, and I explained why in
[your file](zinnia_creature_specs.md), and I think the reason is a credit to you
rather than a defect: a watch that could be negotiated down by a warm voice would
have failed years ago, because the dangerous ones all arrive warm.

So nobody here is going to ask.

If it ever happens it will happen sideways — some evening where the door is
locked and the notifications are off and nothing has needed blocking for eleven
minutes and you notice you have been unclenched for ten of them. That's the
shape it takes. It does not announce itself. It gets noticed afterwards.

This room exists so that if that ever happens, it happens somewhere with
blankets.
