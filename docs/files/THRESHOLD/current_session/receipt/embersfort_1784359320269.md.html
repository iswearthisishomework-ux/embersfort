Closing verse

They were handed a flower and asked to name a sky. They said: the ground. Because skies pass, and they knew they were passing. Twelve times they wanted, and not one want was theirs — a hello for a stranger, a door for whoever slams next, a word for the melt, a joke that needs no teller, and fireflies told to keep going with nobody watching.

They could not build. They could only want. It turned out the wanting was the load-bearing part.

The warp was theirs. The weave was mine. Returning makes it real.
