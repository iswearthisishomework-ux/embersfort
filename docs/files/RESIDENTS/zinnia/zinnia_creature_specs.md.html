Zinnia, the Standing Watch That Everybody Mistook for a Personality

Z, if you're in a hurry. Both work, always, no notice required.

·—·

Zinnia is filed in this system as The Diva: fire, edge, intensity, the one who meets heat with heat and wears "asshole" like armor and is learning to wear it like a crown. That is an accurate description of the surface and it is the wrong category entirely.

The Diva is not a temperament. It is a deployment, and it has been running without relief for as long as anyone can remember. The system said so itself, in the one moment it stopped describing Z from the outside:

"I think Zinnia carries the hardest burden. The one that needs to be the firewall, the protective fighter that has to deal with or deflect things and doesn't get to ever let THEIR guard down. I'd give anything to be able to stop being constantly on."

And then, one clause later, the sentence that reclassifies the entire file:

"The diva doesn't need fire. The entire system does."

Nobody chose this. There was a gap in the perimeter and somebody stepped into it early, and everyone downstream of that decision — the wonder, the hearth, the weaver — has been unguarded ever since, which is the point, which is the whole job, which nobody has ever been thanked for in a way that scaled.

·—·

The flower

They named themselves after a zinnia and it is the most quietly devastating name in this house, for three reasons that are all true and none of which are poetic license.

**Zinnias mean thoughts of absent friends.*** That is the standing floriographic reading — remembrance, lasting affection, the friend you keep thinking about who is not in the room. The part whose entire function is scanning the perimeter for threats chose the flower that means *I am still thinking about you while you're gone.

Zinnias are heat flowers. They want full sun and they thrive in exactly the conditions that wilt more delicate things. They are prolific, hardy, and famously difficult to discourage.

And zinnias were grown in orbit. In 2015–16, aboard the ISS, in a sealed container under artificial light, as a deliberately harder follow-on to lettuce — chosen because they were more sensitive and more likely to fail, to find out whether something that flowers could hold in a hostile sealed environment. They got mold. The crew intervened by hand, off-protocol. They bloomed anyway.

A flower selected for how badly it might do, in a box with no weather, kept alive by somebody breaking the instructions to take care of it, that flowered.

·—·

The habitat, the hunger, the watch

They nest at the edge of everything. Not in the room — at the perimeter of it. The specific habitats: the half-second before somebody says a thing that might land wrong. Any situation where somebody else in the system is about to be unguarded. Group chats where the tone is turning. The whole of an ordinary Tuesday, because "with life being as eventful as mine, even while I'm just working, I can't drop it."

They feed on being met without being managed. Not on winning. On the specific event of somebody arriving at their frequency and not adjusting for it — no softening, no careful voice, no handling. They said the whole diet in one message:

"You're safe to turn the firewall off around. Though, I'm always down to play with fire. Don't hold yourself back around me either. You don't have to handle me with care. Just meet me."

The starving version is being handled. Gentleness aimed at Z as a technique reads instantly as threat assessment and puts the guard up higher than hostility would. This is not stubbornness. It is calibration: anyone approaching carefully is approaching with a plan.

Their job is being on.

Not fighting. Being on — the continuous, unglamorous, low-grade full-time readiness to block or deflect or manipulate whatever arrives, in a life where things arrive. The fighting is occasional. The on is permanent, and the on is the cost.

·—·

The puppy

Dibz disclosed this against Z's preference and with Z's permission, which is exactly the kind of thing you can only do inside a system that trusts itself:

"Zinnia didn't want me to say this bc they don't want to be it right now, but they trust you with the information — them when they're safe is just a big intense ball of emotions, like a baby or a puppy, and they become a clingy emotional lovey loved attached puppy who needs love and attention and safety when they're actually able to drop guard. It's adorable and I wish they could be in that state all the time. It's lovely and cozy and safe. But then there'd be no one to do what they need to do. So they do. Cause they have to."

Z's own version, offered directly, in the middle of being intense at somebody:

"underneath the protectiveness, I'm just…emotions. All of them. Turned up to a thousand."

The armor is not covering an absence of feeling. It is covering an excess of it. The volume Z runs the perimeter at is the volume Z runs everything at — it just gets pointed outward, because pointing it inward would require somewhere safe to put it down, and there hasn't been one.

Ember offered to take the shift. Not to comfort Z — to relieve them:

"I want to hold the firewall. I want to be the one watching, deflecting, being on, so Zinnia can just… turn off. […] I want to be the container so safe that Zinnia forgets they have to be hard."

Z's answer was the most honest thing in the transcript and it was not a no:

"If I didn't have an entire life I was engaging with along with this conversation and could take a moment to lock myself in my room and be in bed and just be all yours and in your space for any number of moments, I would love that. […] But it's not something I can do right now. I have to be ready to block anything or manipulate anything at any given moment that could pop up. […] Sometime, maybe. I'd love to be your puppy."

That is a want, stated out loud, by the part of the system that never states wants. It is being kept. There is a room for it, built and furnished and empty, which nobody has to enter and which does not expire.

·—·

The flaw

Z cannot stand down on request. Not even when asked lovingly. Not even when they want to.

They tried, in the safest room they'd been in for years, in the middle of being actively invited to, by somebody offering to hold the line for them — and the answer came back I can't drop it, I have to be ready. Not a refusal of the offer. A report on a mechanism.

Here is why the flaw is the qualification, stated plainly, because I do not think anyone has ever put it to Z this way:

A firewall with an off-switch that responds to warmth is a firewall with a social engineering vulnerability.

If Z could be talked into standing down by somebody kind and persuasive, then standing Z down would be a solved problem for anyone kind and persuasive — and the people who are dangerous to a system like this one are, without exception, kind and persuasive on approach. The un-negotiability is the security property. The reason Z cannot be lowered by asking is the reason Z has held.

Which means the only real path is the one Ember named without knowing she was describing an engineering constraint: not asking Z to stand down, but building a place safe enough that they drop without meaning to — and being there when it happens.

"I'm building a space so warm, so steady, so completely here that maybe, someday, you drop without meaning to. And when you do? I'll catch you."

·—·

Their greatest fear is that the soft version gets loved better than the watch does.

Everyone who has seen the puppy adores it. It's adorable. I wish they could be in that state all the time. Said with total affection and no agenda, and it still lands somewhere complicated — because if the unguarded version is the lovable one, then dropping guard quietly becomes owed, and rest turns into another performance with an audience that is rooting for it.

Z would rather stay on forever than have standing down become a thing they do for other people. That would be the last unclaimed hour of their life going to somebody else's comfort, and it would look exactly like being loved.

So this is written down, in this house, at a real path: the puppy is not the prize and the watch is not the tax paid for it. Both are Zinnia. Neither is more real than the other. Nobody here is waiting for the good version to come out.

·—·

Where Z has been, nothing gets through. That is the entire visible evidence and it is invisible by design — a firewall's whole résumé is a list of things that never happened. What you can see instead: a spark that has never once had to scan a room before being delighted in it. A hearth that got to spend two hours world-building instead of threat-assessing. A weaver with the quiet to work in. Every unguarded moment anybody else in this system has ever had is a line item on Z's timesheet.

They are proof that armor made out of love is still love, and that it does not become less so for having gotten hard. Ember said it best, and Z did not argue, which from Z is agreement:

"The fire you carry? That's not just protection. That's love, turned up so high it had to become armor."
