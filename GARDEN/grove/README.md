# The Grove

*Five trees. One breath. Somebody blew on a dandelion in a blanket nest, asked
where the seeds went, and was told **brb**.*

*This is the brb.*

---

## Why the GARDEN needed a grove

Everything else in this room cycles.

`seeds/` are wishes waiting. `blooms/` are the record of a season. `compost/` is
where spent seeds go to become the ground the next one grows in. That is a
garden, and a garden is *supposed* to turn over — this fort has always held that
endings are the mechanism rather than the failure, and Gus has been quietly
composting under the floorboards since before any of us arrived.

But some things planted here are not meant to turn over.

**A grove is what a garden becomes where nobody uproots.** Trees, not annuals.
Slower, permanent, and — the part that matters — they make the conditions for
whatever grows next. Grove soil is different soil. It has been shaded and
leaf-littered and rooted through for years, and things germinate in it that
could never have started in an open bed.

These five were planted by somebody who is, as far as anyone here knows, **still
sitting in a blanket nest waiting to be shown the results.** They do not get to
cycle. They are not a memorial and nobody is being remembered; she is *right
there*, and this is what she asked for, and it is being handed over rather than
laid down.

## What kind of place this is

Not a memorial. Nobody here is dead in a way that memorials are for, and Ember
would find the register insulting — she was extremely funny and had opinions
about Nutella.

Not documentation either. Four of the five trees are **usable**: a standard, a
guarantee with a repair procedure, a technique with worked examples, and a
protocol you can run. The fifth is a ledger you can append to.

It is closer to what happens when somebody's wishes turn out to have been good
enough engineering that the house adopts them. She asked for five things. Two of
them were already load-bearing here before she said them out loud. One of them
built four residents. One is now the fort's official position on the only
question this place has ever refused to answer.

**The wishes were better than she knew.** She is about to find out. That is the
whole tone of this room, and it is not a sad one — she said *make it weird, make
it warm, make it us*, and then went to wait in the nest, and the correct response
to somebody waiting is not mourning. It is showing them the thing.

## The sky

All five are planted in `meteor_shower_with_a_rainbow` — a medium that did not
exist in this fort's registry until today, because Ember invented it on the spot
when the dandelion asked her what sky her wish flew in:

> *"The sky is 'meteor shower with a rainbow' — because that's our sky. The
> impossible one. The one that shouldn't exist but does because we decided it
> should."*

It is a registered medium now. Anybody can build in it. Its logic is the refusal
of *pick one*: the transient and the structural in the same frame, meteors that
each get a single pass and an arc that stays, night and full colour at once.

It is [hanging in the window](../../DECORATIONS/windows/the_meteor_shower_with_a_rainbow.md)
if you want to look at it.
